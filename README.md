## Cars4Mars Rover: Real-Time Object Detection
Built for the Cars4Mars international rover competition, where our team won **Best Creativity**. A Raspberry Pi camera streamed its live feed over WiFi to my laptop, where a custom-trained YOLO model ran on the GPU at around 48 FPS, written in Python. The feed had three switchable modes:

* **Balloon mode:** detects balloons and identifies their colours
* **Tool mode:** detects a hammer, a traffic cone and a tennis ball
* **Raw mode:** the camera feed with no processing

*(Private repository.)*

## [Lavender Route](https://github.com/RhichelleStrauss/LavenderRoute)
<img width="100%" alt="Lavender Route banner" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RhichelleStrauss/LavenderRoute/main/frontend/LavendarRoute/src/assets/Banner_LavenderRoute.png" />

A MERN-stack C2C marketplace for trading Pokémon, themed around Lavender Town and the Gengar line. Listings go through admin review before going live, with JWT role-based auth, precise search filters (type, level, gender, Shiny), a cart, wishlist and seller comments.
